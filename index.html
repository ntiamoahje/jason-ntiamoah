Featured Film — “you’re just a ghost”

Primary visual work exploring emotional distance, repetition, and dissociation.

“You’re just a ghost” is an experimental visual piece centered on emotional absence and the feeling of being physically present but mentally elsewhere. Built around repetition, negative space, and sound-led pacing, the project leans into restraint instead of over-explaining, letting the viewer sit inside the disconnect.
