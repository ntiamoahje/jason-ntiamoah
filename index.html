Capstone Media Theses Production

Assistant Editor / Production Support • Experimental Short Film

An experimental short film exploring dissociation through repetition, uncanny imagery, and distorted sound design. Rather than following a traditional three-act structure, the project immerses the audience in derealization—blurring reality and dreamlike perception.

As part of the production team, I assisted with editing, visual continuity, and sound-driven storytelling. The goal is not to explain dissociation, but to make the audience feel it viscerally. Developed for professional evaluation and capstone-level expectations in production workflow and post-production execution.
