Assistant Editor / Production Support • Experimental Short Film

An experimental short film exploring dissociation through repetition, uncanny imagery, and distorted sound design. Rather than following a traditional three-act structure, the film immerses the audience in the sensation of derealization and emotional detachment.

As part of the production team, I assisted in editing, visual continuity, and sound-driven storytelling. The goal is not to explain dissociation, but to make the audience feel it viscerally. Developed for professional evaluation at the capstone level.
